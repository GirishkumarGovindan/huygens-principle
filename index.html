1. Core Postulates of Huygens' Wave Theory

  • Wavefront: The locus of all points in a medium that vibrate with the same phase.
  • Secondary Disturbance: Each point on a primary wavefront acts as a fresh source of secondary disturbance, sending out spherical wavelets with speed $v$ in all forward directions.
  • Forward Envelope: The forward surface tangential to all these secondary spherical wavelets at a subsequent time $\tau$ forms the new position of the wavefront.
