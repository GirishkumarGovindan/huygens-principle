Class 12 Physics • Chapter 10: Wave Optics

1. Huygens' Principle Postulates

  1. Each point on a primary wavefront acts as a source of secondary spherical wavelets spreading out with the speed of light in that medium.
  2. The forward tangential surface (envelope) touching these wavelets at time $t$ gives the new position of the wavefront.

2. Huygens' Derivation of Laws of Reflection

Let a plane wavefront $AB$ be incident at angle $i$ on reflecting surface $XY$ at time $t = 0$. Let $v_1$ be the velocity of light in the medium, and let $\tau$ be the time taken for point $B$ to reach point $C$.

From the geometry of the triangle:

$$BC = v_1 \tau$$

During time $\tau$, the secondary wavelet from point $A$ spreads to radius $AE = v_1 \tau$. The tangent plane $CE$ represents the reflected wavefront.

In right-angled triangles $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta AEC$:

$$\angle ABC = \angle AEC = 90^\circ$$
$$AC = AC \quad (\text{common hypotenuse})$$
$$BC = AE = v_1 \tau$$

By RHS congruence, $\Delta ABC \cong \Delta AEC$.

$$\angle BAC = \angle ECA \implies i = r$$

Hence, the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection ($i = r$), proving the first law of reflection.

3. Huygens' Derivation of Snell's Law of Refraction

Let light travel from medium 1 ($v_1$, $n_1$) into medium 2 ($v_2$, $n_2$). The incident wavefront $AB$ touches boundary $XY$ at $A$ at time $t = 0$. In time $\tau$, edge $B$ reaches $C$, so $BC = v_1 \tau$.

In time $\tau$, the secondary wavelet originating from $A$ travels in medium 2 to a radius $AE = v_2 \tau$.

From right-angled $\Delta ABC$:

$$\sin i = \frac{BC}{AC} = \frac{v_1 \tau}{AC}$$

From right-angled $\Delta AEC$:

$$\sin r = \frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{v_2 \tau}{AC}$$

Taking the ratio:

$$\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{\frac{v_1 \tau}{AC}}{\frac{v_2 \tau}{AC}} = \frac{v_1}{v_2} = \frac{c / v_2}{c / v_1} = \frac{n_2}{n_1} = n_{21}$$
$$n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r \quad (\text{Snell's Law})$$

This proves Snell's Law of Refraction and directly establishes that when light enters an optically denser medium ($n_2 > n_1$), its speed decreases ($v_2 < v_1$), bending the ray towards the normal.
